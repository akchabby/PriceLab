# Resume-ready project entry

**PriceLab — Pricing & Revenue Optimization Engine**  
*Python, PostgreSQL, SQL, scikit-learn, Streamlit, Plotly*

- Built an interactive pricing simulator that forecasts product demand, revenue, gross profit, and margin under changes to price, promotions, competitor pricing, and marketing spend.
- Developed interpretable product-level demand models to estimate price elasticity and identify profit-maximizing prices across 8 retail products and 4,000+ daily observations.
- Designed a PostgreSQL data layer with validation rules, performance indexes, a repeatable ingestion pipeline, and SQL reporting queries for pricing and product performance analysis.
- Created an executive dashboard that translates model outputs into pricing recommendations, performance trends, and measurable business impact.
